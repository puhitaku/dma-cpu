# Phase 25: true 480p

The user: "I'm not going to make use of ramdisk but just redirect
echo to showcase the write access. By minimizing ramdisk and arena,
the framebuffer's vertical resolution can be pushed up to 480 px."
Correct on all counts — the arithmetic just had two surprises.

## The squeeze (Feather only; the RP2040 hasn't the SRAM by half)

640x480x8 = 300 KiB, twice the old row-doubled framebuffer. Paid
for by: the RAM disk down to 10 KiB (16 inodes — 64 inodes cost
four blocks, real weight at this size; enough for the echo-redirect
write showcase, since RAM-disk writes never depended on flash
persistence), the scanout working area down to one page (the
CPU feeder needs only the pan word — the 25 KiB "ring area" was a
DMA-scanout fossil), idle and the disk tucked into sh-data's slack,
and the arena cut to 69 KiB.

## The two surprises

1. The largest exec was no longer the toolbox: fbtools (fbtest +
   show + deck, 49 KiB) was, and show is the binary a presentation
   RUNS. Split three ways on the user's criterion — toolbox keeps
   the pure-xv6 tools, hwtools takes gpio/mux/blink (only
   meaningful on an RP2 pad), fbtest and show stand alone. Largest
   instance now 39 KiB.
2. The arena's invisible residents: init plus sh's HEAP. umalloc's
   morecore rounded every first ask up to 4096 units — 32 KiB of
   resident heap for a shell that uses a fraction of it, most of
   the 480p arena. The minimum ask is now 512 units; the kernel's
   ksbrk still grants its 16 KiB HEAPCHUNK.

## Geometry, everywhere at once

One row per scanline now: the feeder drops its `line >> 1` (FIFO
bandwidth is unchanged — it always pushed every scanline), kfb/
kfbcon go to 480 rows / 60 text rows, sldgen renders square pixels
(no more 1:2 anamorphic integration), and show row-doubles any
slide that fills exactly half the framebuffer, so pre-480p decks
keep working (re-convert for native sharpness and to skip the
interpreted expand).

Silicon: 21/21 HIL PASS at 300 MHz, fbtest paints the full-height
test card, `free` reports the 69 KiB arena, echo > note works on
the 10 KiB disk, and the old deck displays row-doubled.
